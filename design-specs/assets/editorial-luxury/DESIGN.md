## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, tactile digital workspace tailored to Senior Advocates, corporate counsels, and premier chambers within the Indian legal ecosystem. The visual language rejects the cold, homogenized blue-and-purple conventions of generic legal tech in favor of an editorial atmosphere that honors legal heritage while delivering modern AI capabilities.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **Architectural Tactility**. The experience mirrors holding fine archival bond paper, bespoke vellum bindings, and polished brass library markers. High-level reasoning, case precedents, and statutory intelligence are elevated through deliberate typographic discipline, generous breathing space, structured Roman numeral indices, and physical restraint. The emotional response is one of supreme composure, scholarly gravitas, and quiet confidence.

## Colors

The palette relies on deliberate architectural proportions: approximately 70% warm atmospheric backgrounds, 20% deep calligraphic ink tones, 7% tactile bronze and espresso mid-tones, and 3% purposeful terracotta and gold accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Surfaces & Canvas (70%):** The primary canvas rests on Warm Ivory (`#F5F1EA`), flanked by Warm White (`#FAF8F4`) for raised card sheets and Soft Stone (`#E3DED6`) for layered substrate containers, recessed zones, and tab rails.
- **Deep Contrast & Readability (20%):** Deep Ink (`#11100F`) serves as the foundational text and primary structural color, softened slightly by Espresso (`#211B17`) for navigational headers and Warm Brown (`#49382C`) for dense statutory citations.
- **Support & Metadata (7%):** Muted Brown (`#756A60`) provides secondary typography, metadata stamps, and subtle structural outlines. Bronze (`#9A7047`) and Antique Gold (`#C19A5B`) denote institutional authority, active statutory citations, and verification badges.
- **Interactive Accents (3%):** Burnt Terracotta (`#B65F2A`) acts as the primary interactive catalyst for affirmative calls to action and critical analysis indicators. Deep Burgundy (`#4A1F1B`) is reserved for adverse case precedents, critical risk alerts, and high-impact analytical disclaimers.

Saturated hues, blue primary links, and electronic gradients are strictly prohibited.

## Typography

Typography establishes the architectural spine of the product. The pairing balances the classical literary authority of **Playfair Display** with the neutral, hyper-legible precision of **Manrope**.

- **Editorial Headings (Playfair Display):** Conveys the gravity of legal jurisprudence. Used exclusively for case names, primary document titles, section indices, and executive brief summaries. All mobile display titles must use restrained sizing to prevent excessive line wrapping in dense citation strings.
- **Operational & Analytical Copy (Manrope):** Delivers clarity across case summaries, statutory citations, metadata markers, and AI rationale notes. Increased line-height across `body-lg` and `body-md` ensures sustained reading comfort under prolonged legal research sessions.
- **Section Labeling & Metadata:** All operational labels, taglines, and metadata markers utilize small uppercase styling (`label-md` and `label-sm`) with tracking expanded between `+0.08em` and `+0.1em`. Editorial section markers leverage classical numbering schemas (e.g., `01`, `02`, or Roman numerals `§ I`, `§ II`) styled with `numeral-section`.

## Layout & Spacing

Designed primarily for mobile legal workflows, the layout enforces an uncluttered, single-column reading column that mimics high-grade legal folios.

- **Canvas Grid:** Built upon a 4-column fluid mobile grid anchored by `margin` (`1.25rem` / 20px) on outer screen boundaries and `gutter` (`1rem` / 16px) for interior split controls (such as dual action buttons or side-by-side precedent counters).
- **Rhythm & Cadence:** Generous top-to-bottom margins preserve breathing room around core legal arguments. Content blocks, statutory holdings, and AI synthesis threads are segregated through deliberate vertical intervals (`space-xl`) paired with hairline rules rather than heavy boxes.
- **Structural Partitioning:** Sections begin with an editorial index (e.g., `01 / SUMMARY OF ARGUMENTS`) followed by a strict vertical sequence: 8px (`space-sm`) to title, 12px (`space-md`) to divider, and 20px (`space-lg`) to the body narrative.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through tactile surface layering, warm low-contrast outlines, and ambient physical shadowing rather than digital elevation ramps.

- **Warm Architectural Outlines:** The primary separation method uses a crisp, single-pixel hairline rule: `rgba(33, 27, 23, 0.1)`. This replaces standard high-contrast borders and gives cards the presence of hand-cut stationary.
- **Tonal Substrates:** Stacking indicates priority. The base viewport sits on Warm Ivory (`#F5F1EA`). Active workspace modules, case card sheets, and legal briefs lift onto Warm White (`#FAF8F4`). Inactive, collapsed, or secondary argument panels rest slightly recessed in Soft Stone (`#E3DED6`).
- **Warm Ambient Shadows:** Floating interface elements (such as bottom query sheets, filter drawers, or floating action bars) project an ambient, warm-tinted diffusion:
  - *Standard Card:* `0 1px 3px rgba(33, 27, 23, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(33, 27, 23, 0.03)`
  - *Elevated Drawer / Active Tool:* `0 8px 24px rgba(33, 27, 23, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(33, 27, 23, 0.04)`

## Shapes

The shape system employs disciplined, moderate corner curves that balance modern digital software with the structure of bound legal ledgers.

- **Corner Curvature:** The base radius is defined at `roundedness: 2`. Standard cards, interactive input fields, and brief containers utilize `rounded-lg` (12px on mobile screens, scaling down to 8px for inner components).
- **Control Geometry:** Secondary tags, status flags, and badge pills leverage `rounded-sm` (4px) to retain an archival, stamped aesthetic. Full circle pills are reserved strictly for circular icon actions (such as vocal dictation or bookmark buttons).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Burnt Terracotta or Ink):** Styled with solid `#B65F2A` (or `#11100F` for definitive final actions), sharp uppercase typography (`label-md`), 14px vertical padding, and 10px corner radius. No gradient overlays. Active tap produces a subtle depth compression via `scale(0.98)`.
- **Secondary Action (Warm Ivory Sheet):** Background in `#FAF8F4` bounded by a 1px border of `rgba(33, 27, 23, 0.2)`. Label set in `#211B17`.
- **Tertiary / Text Action:** Ghost container with Warm Brown (`#49382C`) or Bronze (`#9A7047`) text, underlined with a 1px baseline offset by 4px.

### Cards & Legal Brief Modules
- Encased in Warm White (`#FAF8F4`) with a 1px `rgba(33, 27, 23, 0.1)` perimeter and a 10px corner radius.
- Cards lead with an editorial index header: a top row containing the classification tag (e.g., `SC / CONSTITUTION BENCH`) in `label-sm` alongside an editorial index marker (`01`) separated by a hairline divider from the case title.

### Input Fields & Legal Prompts
- Background fills utilize `#FAF8F4` framed with a subtle 1px line of `rgba(33, 27, 23, 0.15)`.
- Focused state transitions border color smoothly to Bronze (`#9A7047`) without a blue glow ring. Placeholders use Muted Brown (`#756A60`) in `body-md` italic.

### Chips & Precedent Tags
- Rectangular with a slight 4px radius. 
- *Statute Applied:* Background `#E3DED6` with text `#49382C`.
- *Overruled / Cautionary:* Background `rgba(74, 31, 27, 0.08)` with text Deep Burgundy (`#4A1F1B`).
- *Good Law / Affirmed:* Background `rgba(154, 112, 71, 0.1)` with text Bronze (`#9A7047`).

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- Custom square selectors with a 2px micro-curve. Unselected: 1.5px border of `#756A60` on a `#FAF8F4` canvas. Selected: filled `#11100F` with an Antique Gold (`#C19A5B`) interior check glyph.

### Bespoke System Component: Citation Verifier Bar
- An anchored mobile rail summarizing case health: a hairline progress split dividing *Followed*, *Distinguished*, and *Overruled* precedents using Deep Burgundy, Soft Stone, and Antique Gold segments, topped with tracked small-caps metadata.

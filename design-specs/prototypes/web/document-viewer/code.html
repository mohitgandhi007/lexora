SUPREME COURT ARCHIVES

IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA

EXTRAORDINARY APPELLATE JURISDICTION

SPECIAL LEAVE PETITION (CIVIL) DIARY NO. 41920 OF 2024

(Under Article 136 of the Constitution of India)

IN THE MATTER OF:

Union of India, through Ministry of Mines & Anr. ... Petitioners
Versus
K.S. Minerals Private Limited ... Respondent

SYNOPSIS AND LIST OF DATES

1. The present Special Leave Petition under Article 136 of the Constitution of India is directed against the final Judgment and Order dated 14.08.2024 passed by the Hon'ble High Court of Delhi in FAO (OS) (COMM) No. 84 of 2023, whereby the Division Bench erroneously upheld the setting aside of the domestic Arbitral Award dated 17.03.2021 rendered unanimously by the Arbitral Tribunal under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

2. [RATIO CONFLICT DETECTED] The impugned judgment enters impermissibly into the merits of the factual appreciation made by the Sole Arbitrator regarding soil moisture escalation under Clause 18.3, squarely in contravention of the law laid down by this Hon'ble Court in Associate Builders v. DDA (2015) 3 SCC 49 and Ssangyong Engineering & Construction Co. Ltd. (2019) 15 SCC 131, wherein it was authoritatively settled that patent illegality cannot be invoked to review pure factual findings or rewrite commercial contracts.

gavel SCC Online Cited • 98.4% Judicial Concurrence
Section 34(2A) Standard

3. That the High Court completely lost sight of the settled position that an arbitral tribunal is the final judge of the quality as well as quantity of evidence. The construction placed by the arbitrator upon the correspondence dated 12.02.2019 was a plausible construction and, therefore, beyond the supervisory purview of Section 37 appellate jurisdiction.

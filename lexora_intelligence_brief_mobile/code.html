01 / Synthesized Argumentation

Executive Summary

The instant Special Leave Petition impugns the judgment of the Division Bench of the High Court of Judicature at Bombay, which erroneously upheld an award passed in total disregard of the explicit statutory covenants.

The respondent company was granted a 20-year concession under Clause 14.2 Concession Agreement for limestone extraction. Following unseasonal monsoon flooding in 2021, the respondent defaulted on guaranteed minimum royalties. The Sole Arbitrator stepped outside arbitral bounds by rewriting commercial consideration under the guise of an equitable compromise.

balance Dispute Genesis & Core Illegality

Prolonged adverse weather in 2021 was weaponized by the arbitrator to substitute an equitable formula ex aequo et bono without express bilateral authorization, committing a fatal Section 34(2A) patent illegality.
